About Gaming Hub

A small independent project built around simple, original browser games and a welcoming place to play.

What is Gaming Hub?

Gaming Hub is an independent browser-game project. It brings together short mini-games that visitors can play directly in a web browser without creating an account or installing a game app.

The project is designed to be easy to explore on a computer, tablet or phone. Its games include quick reflex challenges, puzzles, sports-style timing games and classic arcade ideas.

Our approach

  • Simple to start: game instructions are shown before or during play.
  • Original mini-games: the playable games on this site are created for Gaming Hub.
  • Clear navigation: visitors can browse categories, search games and read about the project.
  • Respect for creators: we aim to use original artwork and content or material we have permission to use.

Independent project notice

Gaming Hub is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any game publisher, console maker or other gaming brand. Game titles and artwork on this site are original project materials unless otherwise stated.
