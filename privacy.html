Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. This page explains the current information practices for Gaming Hub.

1. Information collected by Gaming Hub

The current version of Gaming Hub does not ask visitors to create accounts, enter profile details, submit scores to a server or make payments. Game scores and game progress are used only while a game is running in the visitor's browser and are not sent to a Gaming Hub account.

If you choose to contact the site owner by email, your email provider will process the message and its contents.

2. Hosting and technical information

This website is currently hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including visitor IP addresses, for security and service operation. Please read GitHub's own Privacy Statement for details about its processing.

3. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current version of Gaming Hub does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies and does not currently display third-party advertisements.

If analytics, advertising services such as Google AdSense, embedded content or other third-party tools are added later, this policy must be updated before those tools are enabled. Those providers may use cookies or similar technologies and process information according to their own privacy policies and settings.

4. Children and families

Gaming Hub is designed as a general-audience mini-game project. Visitors should not submit sensitive personal information through the site. Parents or guardians can help younger visitors understand online privacy and safety.

5. External websites

Links to other websites may have their own privacy policies and practices. Gaming Hub is not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website changes. The date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is made. If you have a privacy question, use the Contact page.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, visit the Contact page.
